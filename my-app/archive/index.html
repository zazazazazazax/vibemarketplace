Poorly Drawn Binders was a home for collecting, trading, and a shared love of poorly drawn things. This is what we’re keeping.

A closed chapter

The original marketplace has retired.

After the VibeMarket V1 exploit on Base, we retired the marketplace and kept this space for the art and memories.

The Base marketplace and its trading features are no longer available. This page preserves a small piece of the project: its artwork, its personality, and the time we spent building and collecting together.

This is a static archive. There is no wallet connection, signing, buying, selling, or claiming here, and no live prices or liquidity information.

Retiring this website does not revoke existing on-chain approvals or change deployed contracts. This archive is separate from any new VibeMarket deployment.
